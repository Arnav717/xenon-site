Real-time multi-observer fusion

See every person in the building. Even behind walls.

Xenon turns helmet-mounted iPhones into a single live 3D picture. Each phone localizes the people it sees with LiDAR; the hub fuses every viewpoint into one shared map — so any operator can see a contact another operator spotted, through the walls between them.

Capabilities

One shared picture from many partial views.

No single camera sees the whole building. Xenon makes that irrelevant: every observer contributes what it sees, and every observer gets back the full picture.

01 // X-RAY

Through-wall awareness

People seen by a teammate appear in your AR view as x-ray mannequins, flagged BEHIND WALL when scanned geometry sits between you.

02 // FUSION

Multi-observer tracking

Detections from every phone fuse in one ARWorldMap frame. People keep stable ids as they move between rooms and in and out of each phone's view.

03 // STRUCTURE

LiDAR room capture

Walk the building once with a LiDAR iPhone. RoomPlan captures walls, doors, windows and furniture, then shares the model with every device.

Operations console

The god view, in any browser.

The hub serves a live 3D console: scanned structure, every asset with its view cone, every track with its line of sight — solid when visible, dashed red when a wall is in the way.

  • Assets

    Online state, sensor type (LiDAR or estimated depth) and contact count per phone.

  • Tracks

    Fused 3D position, which assets see each person, and who is occluded from whom.

  • Helmet feed

    Jump into any phone's camera pose and see exactly what that operator sees.

XENON // OPERATIONS
● HELMET FEED // HELMET-A
Body model

Full-body tracks, not dots.

On-device Vision pose plus per-joint LiDAR depth gives a 19-joint 3D skeleton. The hub smooths each joint, holds briefly-lost joints and enforces rigid limb lengths, so mannequins stay stable at full frame rate.

  • On-device

    Apple Vision body pose, up to six people per frame, fully offline.

  • Depth

    LiDAR per joint; torso-length estimate on phones without LiDAR.

  • Filter

    Per-joint smoothing, joint hold and limb constraints at the hub.

Workflow

From empty building to live picture in minutes.

ONCE

Start the hub

Run the Python hub on a laptop. Open the console in any browser on the network.

ONCE

Scan structure

Walk each room with a LiDAR iPhone, then Save & share the model and world map.

Join the map

Every other phone taps Join map and relocalizes into the shared frame.

Operate

Phones stream pose and people. Everyone sees everyone — including through walls.
